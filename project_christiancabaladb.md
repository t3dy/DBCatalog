# ChristianCabalaDB

A knowledge portal documenting the Christian Cabala: its historical figures (Pico, Postel, Ricius, Reuchlin, Knorr von Rosenroth, etc.), scholarly texts and primary sources, modern scholars (François Secret, Chaim Wirszubski, Gershom Scholem, Joseph Dan), a timeline of Christian interpretations of Kabbalah, and a web-accessible portal with summaries and interconnections. Foundational sources secured include Secret's *Les kabbalistes chrétiens de la Renaissance*, 100+ PDFs on Pico, Reuchlin's *De Arte Cabalistica*, and Wirszubski's *Pico della Mirandola's Encounter with Jewish Mysticism*. Planned across three phases: discovery & ingestion, database & wiki, then website.

## Status (2026-06-27)
Phase 1 (Discovery & Ingestion) — folder structure and 185 local files secured; source ingestion and extraction in progress.
