**Card**: An anthology of liberation-minded writing featuring William S. Burroughs, Timothy Leary, Robert Anton Wilson, Aleister Crowley, and related countercultural figures.

## Summary

The converted ebook shows a large anthology structured around rebellion and liberation as psychological and cultural problems.
