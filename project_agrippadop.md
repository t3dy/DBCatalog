# AgrippaDOP

A scholarly comparison site mapping every chapter of Heinrich Cornelius Agrippa's *De occulta philosophia libri tres* across its two major redactions: the Würzburg manuscript draft (W, ca. 1510) and the first printed Cologne edition (K, 1533). It presents a card-based index of all 203 chapters and 4 epistles, colour-coded by how the 1533 text relates to the 1510 draft (New in 1533, Heavily Expanded, Revised & Expanded, Preserved from W, Largely New), each linking to an essay with the Latin title, the Perrone Compagni collation note, and scholarly discussion. Correspondence data is drawn entirely from the critical apparatus of V. Perrone Compagni's Brill 1992 edition; no continuous text is reproduced, only short identification strings.
