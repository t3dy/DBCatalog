# RAW Occult Cards

RAW's occult material is not just a set of references. It is one of the main arenas where he tests how belief systems behave under pressure.

## Crowley / Thelema

**Card**: Official and later commentary repeatedly link RAW to Crowley. The portal should distinguish direct reference, adaptation, and parody rather than folding everything into generic occultism.

## Qabalah / Tree of Life

**Card**: RAW's fiction often uses Qabalistic structure as a symbolic architecture. Discussion of *The Illuminatus! Trilogy* frequently notes the sephirotic logic beneath its chapter structure and imagery.

## Discordianism

**Card**: Online discussion repeatedly identifies RAW with Discordianism, but the portal should preserve the difference between using Discordian material as a satirical lens and becoming a formal doctrinal spokesman.

## Ceremonial Magic

**Card**: RAW is often discussed as someone who read and used ceremonial magic as a research and imagination tool, not as a simple convert. The portal should track practice, parody, and literary repurposing separately.

## Chaos Magick

**Card**: Later internet discussion often retrofits RAW into chaos magick lineages. That relation matters, but it should be treated as a later reception history, not as an original identity claim.

## Occult Parody

**Card**: A key RAW mode. He repeatedly mines occult systems for comedy, narrative energy, and cognitive flexibility, which means the same passage can be both joke and serious inquiry.

## Secret Societies

**Card**: *Illuminatus* is the flagship text here, and the portal should preserve the difference between fictionalized secret society lore and historical esoteric traditions.
