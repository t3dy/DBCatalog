# Illuminatus Routine Cards

The portal uses "routine" in a Burroughs-adjacent sense: a repeated or contagious textual sequence that behaves like a script. A routine may be comic, paranoid, sexual, bureaucratic, occult, media-driven, or initiatory. It is not just a motif; it is a procedure that acts on characters and readers.

## Conspiracy Callback Routine

**Card**: A sign, name, number, organization, or rumor returns in a new context and seems to prove a hidden design. The routine trains pattern recognition while making that recognition unstable. Its failure mode is overreading; its narrative function is propulsion.

## Bureaucratic Control Routine

**Card**: Institutions speak in files, reports, police procedure, surveillance language, or official explanation. The routine converts social authority into mechanical script. It is useful for tracking how the trilogy satirizes state power and expert certainty.

## Initiatory Confusion Routine

**Card**: A character encounters signs that may be nonsense, revelation, hoax, or test. The routine produces a threshold state similar to Chapel Perilous: interpretation becomes compulsory, but no interpretive frame is safe.

## Media Contagion Routine

**Card**: Rumor, spectacle, celebrity, print culture, broadcast logic, or countercultural signal spreads through the text. The routine treats media as an infection system, where information changes behavior whether or not it is true.

## Occult Instruction Routine

**Card**: Esoteric terms, rituals, correspondences, or secret teachings appear in a mode that could be manual, parody, collage, or lure. The portal should ask what instruction is being staged and whether the text undermines its own authority.

## Discordian Reversal Routine

**Card**: A solemn claim is flipped into joke, a joke becomes doctrine, or doctrine reveals itself as a prank. This is a core Discordian operation: hierarchy and meaning survive only long enough to be rearranged.

## Sexual Farce Routine

**Card**: Sexual energy interrupts political, occult, or bureaucratic seriousness. The routine may be comic, liberatory, coercive, or deliberately excessive, so entries should avoid treating sexual farce as automatically emancipatory.

## Catalog Of Secret Names Routine

**Card**: The text accumulates names, orders, factions, signals, and alleged connections until the list itself becomes the experience. The routine mimics encyclopedic knowledge while exposing the absurdity of total explanation.

## Paranoid Detective Routine

**Card**: Investigation generates more reality rather than more certainty. Clues proliferate, suspects multiply, and the detective structure becomes a machine for producing epistemological vertigo.

## Cosmic Joke Routine

**Card**: The narrative lets revelation collapse into comedy without simply canceling the revelation. This routine is central to the portal's tone discipline: the joke may be a defense, a critique, or the form in which insight survives.

## Cut-Up Proximity Routine

**Card**: Voices, registers, genres, and fragments are placed near one another until causality becomes unstable. This is where Burroughs-like influence matters most: not as a one-to-one source claim, but as a method for making control systems audible.
