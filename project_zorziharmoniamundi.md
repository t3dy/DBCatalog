# ZorziHarmoniaMundi

A browse-and-search website of detailed scholarly summaries for all 163 sections of Francesco Zorzi's *De Harmonia Mundi* (*L'armonia del mondo*), in Saverio Campanini's critical Italian edition (Bompiani, Il Pensiero Occidentale, 2010). Zorzi (1466–1540), a Venetian Franciscan and one of the most ambitious Christian Kabbalists of the Renaissance, drew on Scripture, Neoplatonism, Kabbalah, Pythagoreanism, and the Church Fathers to argue for a single harmonic order pervading the divine, angelic, celestial, and human realms. Each of the 163 chunks has a concise index-card summary and a full essay with verbatim excerpts; static site (card grid + per-chunk essay pages) with JSON-backed content.

## Status (2026-06-27)
Live on GitHub Pages; all 163 sections summarized.
