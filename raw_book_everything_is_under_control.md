# Everything is Under Control: Conspiracies, Cults, and Cover-Ups

**Card**: A coauthored conspiracy reference book by Robert Anton Wilson and Miriam Joan Hill. It surveys conspiracies, cults, cover-ups, and the cultural mechanics of belief.

## Summary

The converted PDF confirms that this is a large reference-style work rather than a single narrative argument. It is useful to the portal because it treats conspiracy not only as a subject but as an archival field. Wilson's role is best understood as interpreter and organizer: someone who knows how to turn conspiratorial material into a readable taxonomy of fear, rumor, media, and modern myth.

## Major Themes

- conspiracies as cultural objects
- cults and organized belief
- cover-up narratives
- mass media and suspicion
- cataloging the paranoia field

## Form and Function

This book helps the portal distinguish RAW's satirical use of conspiracy from the broader archive of conspiracy discourse. It is a bridge between literary play and documentary reference.

## Relation to Illuminatus and Counterculture

The book is one of the cleanest sources for the conspiracy section because it situates RAW inside the very culture of suspicion that *Illuminatus!* dramatizes.
