# RAW Counterculture Cards

RAW is consistently presented online and in official bios as a major countercultural writer. These cards isolate the recurring themes that matter most for the portal.

## Anti-Dogmatism

**Card**: The central countercultural value in RAW. He is repeatedly framed as a writer who resists gurus, grand narratives, and certainty itself.

## Playboy / Mass Culture Interface

**Card**: RAW worked as an associate editor at *Playboy*, which matters because it places him at the intersection of mainstream media, literary experimentation, and the era's cultural loosening.

## Underground / Freak Currents

**Card**: Internet discussion frequently positions RAW in the "head" or "freak" currents of the 70s-90s - not as a square academic but as a public intellectual of alternative culture.

## New Age and Esoteric Publics

**Card**: RAW did not stand outside the New Age scene; he moved through it, criticized it, and used it as material for inquiry. Hilaritas notes his lectures at Esalen and similar centers.

## Libertarian and Anarchic Impulse

**Card**: Online discussion often reads RAW as suspicious of authority in a politically libertarian or anarchic way, though the portal should distinguish this from a simple party-line ideology.

## Celebrity / Media Satire

**Card**: RAW often treats celebrity culture, media spectacle, and public image as part of the same epistemic problem as politics and religion.

## Punk / Post-Punk Afterlife

**Card**: Official sources note a punk rock record, and later readers often place RAW in a broader post-punk, DIY, and alt-media genealogy.
