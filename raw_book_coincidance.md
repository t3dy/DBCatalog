# Coincidance: A Head Test

**Card**: A compact essay collection centered on coincidence, cognition, and the kinds of pattern-recognition games RAW loved to stage.

## Summary

This book works as a laboratory of short argument forms. Wilson uses essays and aphorisms to test how minds interpret events, how coincidences acquire meaning, and how easily thought slides from observation into belief. The book is less a systematic theory than a series of head tests - small trials that reveal how a reader thinks.

## Major Themes

- coincidence and pattern
- testing one's assumptions
- short essay form
- playful skepticism
- interpretive self-awareness

## Form and Function

The book is important for the Illuminatus portal because it translates the trilogy's style of noticing into a nonfiction mode. It keeps the reader alert to the difference between pattern and proof.

## Relation to Illuminatus and Counterculture

Its approach aligns strongly with RAW's broader countercultural position: skeptical, comic, and unwilling to let a single interpretive frame dominate the field.
