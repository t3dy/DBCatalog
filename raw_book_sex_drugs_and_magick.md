# Sex, Drugs and Magick: A Journey Beyond Limits

**Card**: An edited RAW volume centered on altered states, occult discourse, and countercultural freedom.

## Summary

This book collects RAW material around freedom, altered consciousness, eroticism, and esoteric experimentation. The title announces the terrain clearly: a zone where bodily experience, ritual language, and cultural rebellion overlap. The book is useful for the portal because it provides a direct bridge between Wilson's public nonfiction and the more overtly occult atmosphere of *The Illuminatus! Trilogy*.

## Major Themes

- altered states
- freedom and experimentation
- occult discourse
- countercultural rebellion
- the limits of respectability

## Form and Function

Because it is an edited collection, the book should be treated as a curated RAW archive rather than a single linear argument. That makes it ideal for a card/page system: the card can orient the reader, while the page can unpack the source mixture and thematic range.

## Relation to Illuminatus and Counterculture

This is one of the strongest links between RAW's fiction, his essay culture, and the wider 1970s alternative scene. It helps the portal show that the trilogy did not emerge in isolation but out of a larger ecology of experimental thought.
