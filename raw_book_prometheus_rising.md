# Prometheus Rising

**Card**: RAW's best-known practical book on consciousness. It combines psychology, counterculture, and training exercises into a manual for changing how one models reality.

## Summary

*Prometheus Rising* is Wilson's clearest attempt to turn his ideas into a structured method. The chapter structure moves through the Thinker and Prover, brain software, and the first several circuits of consciousness, treating the mind as a programmable system with layers of survival, emotion, language, and social adaptation. The book presents a layered model of consciousness and behavior, then uses exercises, thought experiments, and commentary to encourage the reader to notice how habits of perception become habits of belief. Rather than claiming a final theory, it offers a toolkit for mental flexibility.

## Major Themes

- consciousness training
- belief as conditioning
- model-agnostic thinking
- psychological flexibility
- the ethics of not over-believing
- the Eight Circuit model

## Form and Function

The book sits between self-help, speculative psychology, and cultural critique. It is one of the key texts for understanding the RAW persona that later surfaces in *Cosmic Trigger*: playful, skeptical, and committed to changing the way the mind frames experience.

## Relation to Illuminatus and Counterculture

For the Illuminatus portal, this is one of the most important adjacent works because it clarifies Wilson's interest in how symbolic systems seize the mind. The novel's conspiratorial maze makes more sense when read alongside this book's cognitive and behavioral framework.
