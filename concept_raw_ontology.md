# Ontology: RAW Literary, Occult, and Countercultural Field

This ontology defines what the Illuminatus portal should look for when studying Robert Anton Wilson. It is designed to prevent two failures:

1. reducing RAW to a list of book summaries
2. flattening his work into a single interpretive lane

RAW must be treated as a writer whose books, essays, interviews, and public persona operate across several overlapping domains:

- literary method
- occult and esoteric interest
- psychedelic culture interpretation
- counterculture mediation
- Beat and post-Beat lineage
- PKD-adjacent speculative and epistemological inquiry

## 1. Core Interpretive Principle

RAW is not merely an author with a bibliography. He is a writer who repeatedly stages *how interpretation works*.

That means the ontology must preserve:

- form
- voice
- mode of argument
- medium
- cultural network
- esoteric reference
- historical context

It should not collapse all of these into a single generic "theme."

## 2. Primary Ontological Dimensions

### A. Literary Method

This axis tracks how RAW writes.

Look for:
- parody
- satire
- collage
- essayistic digression
- memoir as epistemology
- fragmentary argument
- aphorism
- fiction/nonfiction bleed
- unreliable or self-aware narration
- meta-commentary on belief and interpretation

### B. Occult and Esoteric Interest

This axis tracks RAW's use of occult material as literature, inquiry, and cultural signal.

Look for:
- Thelema
- Crowley
- O.T.O.
- Qabalah
- ceremonial magic
- sigils and correspondences
- initiation language
- secret-society discourse
- ritual framing
- occult parody vs occult seriousness

### C. Psychedelic Culture

This axis tracks RAW as an interpreter of altered states and psychedelic subculture.

Look for:
- psychedelics as epistemic event
- altered perception
- consciousness expansion
- drug culture
- psychonaut memoir
- Timothy Leary / Leary-adjacent thought worlds
- ecstatic experimentation
- paranoia and insight as intertwined states

### D. Counterculture

This axis tracks RAW as a mediator of broader countercultural life.

Look for:
- 1960s/1970s alternative culture
- anti-authoritarianism
- libertarian or anarchic impulses
- underground press
- New Age and occult subcultures
- media skepticism
- anti-dogmatism
- political satire
- public intellectual performance

### E. Beat and Post-Beat Lineage

This axis tracks RAW's relation to the Beats and adjacent literary lineages.

Look for:
- Beat textual energy
- open-form prose
- rejection of academic stiffness
- altered consciousness as literary subject
- bohemian/public intellectual mode
- affinity with countercultural essay and memoir traditions
- continuity and divergence from Kerouac, Ginsberg, Burroughs, and later post-Beat forms

### F. PKD and Paranoid Epistemology

This axis tracks RAW's relation to Philip K. Dick and adjacent paranoid-cognitive inquiry.

Look for:
- reality as unstable or layered
- conspiracy as a meaning machine
- theological uncertainty
- epistemic breakdown
- model conflict
- hallucination vs insight
- literary friendship or influence networks
- thematic overlap with PKD's paranoia, mysticism, and metafiction

## 3. Entity Types

The ontology should support these entity classes:

- **Author**: Robert Anton Wilson as historical person and public intellectual
- **Book**: individual titles with card/page pairs
- **Essay/Article**: shorter prose pieces, columns, public arguments
- **Interview**: video or audio conversations
- **Zine / Magazine Item**: underground or periodical publication context
- **Concept**: model-agnosticism, chaos, synchronicity, initiation, belief, etc.
- **Movement / Scene**: psychedelic culture, counterculture, Beat afterlife, occult subculture
- **Person**: Robert Shea, PKD, Timothy Leary, Burroughs, etc.
- **Textual Method**: collage, parody, memoir, essay, satire, aphorism
- **Occult Reference**: Crowley, Thelema, Qabalah, initiation, ritual systems

## 5. Evidence and Uncertainty

Each claim should carry one of these statuses:

- **Verified**: directly supported by the source in hand
- **Likely**: strong inference from available sources
- **Interpretive**: scholarly reading, not a bare fact
- **Placeholder**: source not yet available or not fully verified

When the archive is incomplete, write:
- `[Need to add more bibliographic detail]`
- `[Need to add more interview detail]`
- `[Need to add more source confirmation]`

Do not invent bibliographic precision just to fill a slot.

## 6. Priority Questions for Reading RAW

Every RAW entry should be assessable through these questions:

1. What literary method is being used?
2. What occult or esoteric material is in play?
3. How is psychedelic culture being interpreted?
4. How does the text mediate counterculture?
5. How does it relate to the Beats and post-Beat prose?
6. What is its relation to PKD-style uncertainty or paranoid epistemology?
7. Is the text parodying, honoring, or repurposing its sources?

## 7. Working Rule for the Portal

Do not treat RAW as a single stable doctrine.

Treat him as a writer whose archive constantly remixes:
- literary form
- speculative thought
- occult material
- psychedelic experience
- social criticism
- comic skepticism

That is the ontology the portal should encode.
