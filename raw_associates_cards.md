# RAW Associate Cards

These cards identify the people most likely to matter in an initial RAW study. They are not all equal in closeness or influence, but they recur in official bios, Hilaritas materials, and online discussion.

## Robert Shea

**Card**: Wilson's coauthor on *The Illuminatus! Trilogy* and the key collaborator for the central fiction. The portal should treat Shea not as a secondary footnote but as the co-architect of the trilogy's comic conspiracy engine.

## Arlen Riley Wilson

**Card**: Wilson's wife and an important life partner in the biography archive. Official materials frame her as part of the personal and family context that sustained the RAW project.

## Timothy Leary

**Card**: A crucial friend and intellectual neighbor in RAW's world. Hilaritas emphasizes Leary as a subject of *The Starseed Signals*, and RAW often treats Leary as part of the larger psycho-spiritual counterculture he was interpreting.

## Philip K. Dick

**Card**: A key literary associate and conceptual neighbor. RAW and PKD are often linked in discussion through paranoia, altered reality, and high-weirdness epistemology, even when the relation is indirect rather than literal collaboration.

## Richard Rasa

**Card**: A central Hilaritas/RAW Trust figure in the posthumous archive. Important for understanding how RAW's legacy is curated and made available in the present.

## Christina Pearson

**Card**: RAW's daughter and a key trustee/curatorial presence in the official archive. She is part of the family and legacy structure that keeps the bibliography and reprints alive.

## John Higgs

**Card**: A later interpreter and biographical frame-setter. His introductions and commentary help translate RAW for a contemporary audience while preserving the sense that he is a thinker of uncertainty rather than a doctrinaire guru.

## Grant Morrison

**Card**: A major later admirer whose blurbs and introductions help position RAW within comics, chaos magic, and contemporary counterculture.

## Alan Moore

**Card**: A major later admirer and reader. Useful as a witness to RAW's influence on magical, literary, and comics culture.

## Nick Herbert

**Card**: A physicist and recurring interlocutor in RAW's orbit, especially useful for the science/epistemology side of the ontology.
