# The Earth Will Shake

**Card**: The first volume of the *Historical Illuminatus Chronicles*, using revolutionary history and secret history to extend the trilogy's concerns into another historical register.

## Summary

This novel begins the Historical Illuminatus cycle by setting conspiracy, politics, and secret history within the terrain of revolution-era Europe. The extracted structure moves through tarot-named parts such as The Fool, The Empress, The Magician, The Priestess, The World, The Hanged Man, and The Devil, which makes the book's historical travel and political motion visibly symbolic as well as geographic. It uses historical fiction not merely as backdrop but as a way of testing how power, myth, and hidden networks behave over time.
