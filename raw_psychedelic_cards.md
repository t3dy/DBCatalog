# RAW Psychedelic Cards

RAW is one of the clearest literary interpreters of psychedelic culture in the late 20th-century counterculture. These cards isolate the recurring ideas the portal should track.

## Reality Tunnels

**Card**: The best-known RAW concept online. A reality tunnel is the model by which a person organizes experience, and internet discussion often treats this as the core of his psychedelic epistemology.

## Model Agnosticism

**Card**: RAW's mature philosophical habit of treating all worldviews as provisional models rather than total truths. This is the bridge between psychedelics and epistemology in his work.

## Timothy Leary and the Eight Circuit Model

**Card**: Hilaritas foregrounds RAW's relationship to Leary, and *Prometheus Rising* is central to the discussion of the Eight Circuit model and consciousness training.

## Synchronicity

**Card**: In online discussion, RAW is repeatedly linked to synchronicity as a way of reading experience without collapsing into dogma. It is often treated as a bridge concept between psychedelics and occult inquiry.

## Altered States as Epistemic Events

**Card**: RAW does not merely report trips. He interprets altered states as occasions for reorganizing perception, memory, and belief.

## Psychedelic Demimonde

**Card**: Discussion often frames RAW as a witness to the California occult/psychedelic demimonde - the loose network of thinkers, artists, and seekers moving between drugs, mysticism, and counterculture.

## Sex, Drugs, and Magick

**Card**: This title is a compact statement of RAW's willingness to put sexual, pharmacological, and occult experimentation in one field of analysis.
