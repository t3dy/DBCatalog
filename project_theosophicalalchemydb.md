# TheosophicalAlchemyDB

A concept-first, scholarly encyclopedia of Rosicrucian and theosophical-alchemical traditions from the 16th–18th centuries, built for scholars, practitioners, and interested readers. It lets users navigate historical Rosicrucian and alchemical ideas, figures, texts, and emblematic traditions through concept-first organization, relational browsing, and rigorous scholarly apparatus. Target scope spans roughly 40–60 concepts, 30–40 figures (Andreae, Dee, Fludd, Ashmole, Swedenborg, etc.), 25–35 texts, 20–40 emblems, a 100+ term dictionary, thematic essays, and modern scholars. It is one of the three alchemy databases that ground the EmblemRoguelike narrative.

Live portal on GitHub Pages; concept-first apparatus in place.
