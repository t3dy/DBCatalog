# Quantum Psychology

**Card**: A compact statement of Wilson's mature thinking about perception, models, and the instability of certainty. It is less a technical psychology book than a guide to mental flexibility.

## Summary

This book extends the model-agnostic method in a more explicitly conceptual register. Wilson argues, in effect, that minds are better understood as systems of provisional models than as containers of fixed truths. The writing moves between psychology, epistemology, and practical advice, showing how people get trapped by their own interpretive habits.

## Major Themes

- provisional models
- epistemic humility
- reframing experience
- the danger of certainty
- psychological pluralism

## Form and Function

Compared with *Prometheus Rising*, this text is less overtly instructional and more reflective. It belongs in the portal as a bridge text: a readable distillation of Wilson's cognitive philosophy, useful for students who need the authorial method in a more compact form.

## Relation to Illuminatus and Counterculture

The book helps explain the epistemic atmosphere of *The Illuminatus! Trilogy*: competing interpretations, unstable frames, and a refusal to settle into a single master narrative. It is one of the best RAW texts for connecting literature to his broader theory of mind.
