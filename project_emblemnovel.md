# EmblemNovel

A graphical text adventure that reimagines the Emblem Knight roguelike as a narrative exploration experience. Each scene is anchored to a real early-modern engraving (from *Atalanta Fugiens*, Cramer's *Emblemata Sacra*, or the *Hypnerotomachia Poliphili*), shown as a backdrop while narrative text and dialogue occupy the foreground; choices trigger state changes, advance quests, and move the player to new emblems. Players pick one of three narrative paths at the start — Nigredo (Dissolution), Coniunctio (Conjunction), or Rubedo (Fixation) — and experience the Great Work through dialogue and choice rather than combat. Vanilla JS engine (`main.js`, `state.js`, `scenes.js`, `ui.js`, `assets.js`); served on port 7432.

## Status (2026-06-27)
Prototype skeleton complete (scene engine, state, persistence, basic narrative); content (30+ scenes, NPC dialogue trees, quests) still TBD.
