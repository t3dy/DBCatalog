# Masks of the Illuminati

**Card**: A historical novel that uses secret societies and intellectual history to explore revelation, deception, and the layered nature of truth.

## Summary

This novel shifts RAW's conspiratorial imagination into a more historical register. Instead of contemporary chaos, the book works through secret-society folklore, historical atmosphere, and the relationship between hidden knowledge and public narratives. The result is both literary and historiographical: a novel about how people imagine the past and how the past is used to authorize present belief.

## Major Themes

- secret societies
- revelation and concealment
- historical imagination
- parody and seriousness
- truth as a masked category

## Form and Function

The book matters to the portal because it extends the Illuminatus universe into historical fiction. It lets us see Wilson working not just with conspiracy satire but with the novelistic apparatus of secret history.

## Relation to Illuminatus and Counterculture

It belongs to the same broad imaginative world as the trilogy, but with a different temporal frame. The book is useful for showing how RAW's esoteric satire adapts when the setting shifts from contemporary conspiracy culture to historical speculation.
