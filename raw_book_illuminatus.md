# Illuminatus!

**Card**: The central novel for the portal itself. A sprawling conspiratorial satire that mixes occult references, political paranoia, sexual farce, and epistemological instability.

## Summary

*The Illuminatus! Trilogy* is the primary narrative field of this portal, but it can also be treated as a single composite book. The converted edition is organized as Book One through Book Five plus appendices, which suits a work that stages conspiracy theory, occult discourse, media absurdity, and revolutionary politics as mutually feeding systems. Its power comes from refusing to stabilize the reader's interpretive position.

## Major Themes

- conspiracy as narrative form
- parody of occult and political systems
- epistemological uncertainty
- anarchic multiplicity
- the comic breakdown of authority
- multipart encyclopedic structure

## Form and Function

The book is deliberately layered and unstable. It contains multiple voices, modes, and tonal registers, which makes it ideal for a card/page structure. The card can orient readers to the central action, while the page can unpack subplots, local routines, and interpretive frameworks.

## Relation to RAW and Counterculture

This is the definitive bridge between Wilson's fiction and nonfiction. It belongs at the center of the RAW archive because the later memoirs and essays constantly circle back to the same methods: questioning authority, multiplying models, and treating reality as a contested field.
