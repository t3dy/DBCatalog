## The Core Concept
A summary of a detailed Reddit post ("Marxian Economics Is Still Not Irrelevant") critiquing the neoclassical bias found in mainstream economics forums. The synthesis defends the ongoing relevance of Marxist economic analysis (specifically the Labor Theory of Value and crisis theory) against contemporary dismissals.

## Key Arguments
1. **The Dismissal of Marx**: Mainstream economic forums often treat Marx purely as a philosopher or sociologist, aggressively rejecting his mathematical economic models.
2. **The Defense**: The synthesis argues that Marxist frameworks are uniquely equipped to explain systemic crises, wealth concentration, and the boom-bust cycle of late capitalism, which neoclassical models often treat as external "shocks."
